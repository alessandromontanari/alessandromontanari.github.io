About Me

I'm a passionate researcher and software engineer specialising in Very-High-Energy Astrophysics with a strong background in Data Science and Web Development.

Currently working as a Postdoc at Laboratoire Leprince-Ringuet, École Polytechnique in Palaiseau, France, I lead the development of critical tools for Cherenkov telescopes, including the NectarCAM Data Quality Monitoring system and AI-powered applications.

My work spans from detecting cosmic phenomena like the Fermi Bubbles to building full-stack applications with AI search capabilities. I have a Ph.D. in Astrophysics and Cosmology from CEA/University Paris Saclay, and I'm fluent in Italian, English, and French.
